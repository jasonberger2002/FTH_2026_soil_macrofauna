This folder includes QGIS layers used for mapping.

Sampling locations (sampling_locations) were obtained by combining field measurements with the Paracou inventory (applying the script sampling_locations.r).

Drainage types (P1_P15_drainages) for P1 and P15 were extracted from soil inventory layers for Paracou provided by Vincent Freycon.

5 meter isolines (5_isolines_fromDTM2023) were created from a 2023 DMT provided by Vincent Freycon.

roadspaths3 includes roads and paths in the Paracou area and was provided by Vincent Freycon.

carbet includes the approximate location of the Paracou field station carbet (created by hand).

Outline of French Guiana was obtained via https://gadm.org/
